Before you build

Keep touch off GPIO9

GPIO9 is the ESP32-C3’s BOOT pin. A stock TTP223 idles low and drives push-pull, so a pad wired there holds the board in the serial bootloader at every reset and the sketch never runs. The firmware defaults to GPIO7.

SSD1306 or SH1106?

1.3" panels are usually SH1106; 0.96" ones are SSD1306. If the picture is shifted sideways or wraps around, set DM_DISPLAY_SH1106 1 in config.h and rebuild.

No network, no accounts

The firmware is entirely offline — no Wi-Fi, no microphone, no cloud and no API keys. Flash it and it just works.

When it won't cooperate

No port shows up in the prompt
Nearly always the cable — plenty of USB-C cables are charge-only. Try another, and plug straight into the computer rather than through a hub.
"Failed to initialize" or it connects then drops
The board isn't in bootloader mode. Unplug, hold BOOT, plug in, release, and try again.
Flashing works but the screen stays dark
Check the OLED's I²C wiring and address (0x3C vs 0x3D), and that it's a 3.3 V part.
The face works but it is silent
Sound goes out over I²S to the MAX98357A, not to a buzzer pin. Check BCLK, LRCLK and DIN, and that the amp’s SD pin is not pulled low.
I want to start clean
Re-run the installer and pick Erase device before installing.
