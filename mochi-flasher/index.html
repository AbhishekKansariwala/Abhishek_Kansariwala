First-boot setup

1. Join the hotspot

After the first boot the robot opens its own access point. Connect to it from your phone or laptop — the captive portal opens automatically.

SSID DasaiMochi-Setup

2. Enter credentials

Give it your Wi-Fi SSID and password, plus any API keys the firmware needs for speech and weather.

3. It reboots

The robot restarts, joins your network and shows its face. Hold the touch button and ask it something.

Keys are stored in the ESP32's own flash. Re-flashing erases them, so you'll redo this step after each update.

When it won't cooperate

No port shows up in the prompt
Nearly always the cable — plenty of USB-C cables are charge-only. Try another, and plug straight into the computer rather than through a hub.
"Failed to initialize" or it connects then drops
The board isn't in bootloader mode. Unplug, hold BOOT, plug in, release, and try again.
Flashing works but the screen stays dark
Check the OLED's I²C wiring and address (0x3C vs 0x3D), and that it's a 3.3 V part.
It never joins my Wi-Fi
The ESP32-C3 is 2.4 GHz only. A 5 GHz-only SSID is invisible to it.
I want to start clean
Re-run the installer and pick Erase device before installing. That wipes stored credentials too.
